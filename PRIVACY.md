# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Crosswords** (the "App", package name `com.ali.souf.crosswords`) handles information when you use the App. The App is provided by Soufiane Chalouh ("we", "us", "our").

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at:
**soufianechalouh@gmail.com**

---

## 1. Information we collect

We do **not** ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any name, email address, phone number, or other personal identifier to use the App. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit your contacts, photos, location, or files.

### Information stored on your device only

The App saves your game progress (solved levels, coins, settings, language preference) **locally on your device** using on-device storage. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you uninstall the App.

### Information collected by third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

- A device advertising identifier (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
- Approximate location based on IP address
- Device information (model, operating system, language)
- Aggregated usage and ad-interaction data

This information is collected and used by Google in accordance with **Google's Privacy Policy**: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

You can opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID through your device settings:
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and California, the App will (where applicable) present a consent prompt before any personalized advertising is shown.

---

## 2. Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions:

- **Audio settings (`MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS`) and microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)** — declared by the audio library used for in-game sound effects. The App **does not record, transmit, or store any audio** from your microphone.
- **Internet access** — required to load advertisements and check connectivity.

---

## 3. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us at the email above and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

## 4. How information is used

Information described above is used to:

- Save your game progress on your device.
- Show advertisements that help support the App, including (where you have consented) personalized ads.
- Keep the App functional, secure, and free from abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- Game progress stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage in your device settings.
- Information collected by Google AdMob is retained and deleted in accordance with Google's policies. To request deletion of AdMob-collected data, follow the instructions at <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/10495756>.

We rely on the security mechanisms provided by your device and by Google AdMob. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
